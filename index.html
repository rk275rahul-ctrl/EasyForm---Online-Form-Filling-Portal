Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Find quick answers to common questions about filling and submitting forms on EasyForm.

What documents are required to fill out a form?
Typically, you will need a clear passport-size photograph (JPG/PNG, up to 2MB), a digital specimen signature (up to 1MB), an ID proof such as Aadhaar Card or Passport (PDF/JPG, up to 3MB), and your relevant educational marksheet or certificate.
How do I track my submitted application status?
Once you submit an application, you are issued a unique Reference Number (e.g. EF-2026-89104). You can track your real-time status (Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Approved, or Rejected) anytime by navigating to "My Dashboard".
Can I save my form and continue filling it later?
Yes! At Step 5 (Review & Submit) or during the form, you can click "Save as Draft". Your form will be stored in your dashboard where you can resume or edit whenever you are ready.
What if I uploaded the wrong document or missed one?
You can navigate to "My Dashboard" > "Upload Missing Documents", select your application, and attach additional files. The reviewing administrator will be notified automatically.
How do I print or download my official acknowledgement slip?
Immediately upon submission, or from your User Dashboard at any time, click the "Download / Print Application" button. This generates a standardized printable slip with all details, photo, and digital signature.
